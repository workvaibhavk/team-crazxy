## What We Believe

### Build before you over-explain.

We would rather have a working prototype than a beautiful description of one.

### Use AI. Understand the code.

AI-assisted development is part of our workflow. We don't consider that a substitute for understanding what we're building.

### Not everything needs AI.

If deterministic logic solves a problem, we use deterministic logic.

If a problem genuinely benefits from reasoning, generation or external intelligence, that's where AI belongs.

### Not everything needs a framework.

Technology is a means, not a status symbol.

### Know your strengths.

The point of a team is not to prove that everyone can do everything.

It's to make the combined output better than what any one person could produce alone.
